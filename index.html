      how to play

      cover — guess the smokedope2016 release from its cover. it starts as 1 pixel, then 2×2, 4×4, 8×8, 16×16, 32×32.

      song — guess the song from a clip. you get 0.5s, then 1s, 3s, then 5s (capped).

      easy plays the hook. hard plays a random part of the song instead — never the chorus. on covers, hard is black & white until the round ends. hard has its own daily puzzle and stats.

      unlimited hard also mixes in deep cuts and extra covers you won't see anywhere else.

      6 tries. skipping uses a try.

      close: same project (song) / same type (cover)

      🏆 leaderboards — see how everyone did on today's dailies, and who's played the most unlimited rounds (easy and hard).

      daily resets at midnight. unlimited is endless practice with its own stats — press enter after a round to jump to the next one.
